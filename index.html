The
Witch.

Soft face. Sharp boundaries.

I am a mathematician who never stopped being fascinated by things that cannot be measured.

A few things I believe
I — Nature

Wildness is not chaos.

I find something deeply beautiful in things that grow without asking permission. Forests, bodies, seasons, curls, desire.

II — Mathematics

Everything has a pattern.

Mathematics taught me to look closely. To notice structure, rhythm, repetition, asymmetry — and the strange beauty hidden inside all of it.

III — Eros

Wanting deserves time.

I don't believe everything beautiful needs to be revealed immediately. Some things become more powerful when you let them unfold.

IV — Boundaries

Warmth is not access.

I can be soft without being available to everyone. I can be open without giving everything away.

☽

I don't want to be impossible to resist. I want to be impossible to replace.
